# PatchSpan demo video script (target 3.5-4 min, limit 2-6 min)

Record on the phone the app will be used on: full-screen screen recording, portrait, no notifications (Do Not Disturb on). Show only the app and the fabric/card - no faces, names, school, or personal items in frame. Narrate live or add voiceover after; the lines below are a guide, say them your own way.

## Shot list

**0:00-0:25 - The problem (camera on a real scrap pile, or straight to app)**
- "Tailors and home sewers end up with piles of leftover cloth after every job. Too small for a shirt, too good to throw. Most of it is sold by the kilo or dumped. I built PatchSpan to answer one question: what can I still make from THIS piece?"

**0:25-0:45 - Welcome + pick (screen recording)**
- Open the app. Welcome screen visible ("Every scrap, fitted.").
- Tap "Start with a scrap", pick "Zip pouch".
- "You tell it what you'd like to make - or ask it to suggest something."

**0:45-1:30 - The photo (film the phone over the table, or screen record if already shot)**
- Lay a real scrap on a plain table with any standard ID-size card next to it (use a metro or membership card - never show a real bank card number).
- "You photograph the scrap next to any standard card. The card is the ruler - every one of them is the same size - and the photo never leaves the phone."
- Take the photo from straight above.

**1:30-2:00 - Card corners + trace**
- Tap the card's 4 corners as prompted.
- "Four taps on the card give the app real-world scale."
- Tap "Trace scrap". The outline appears on the photo.
- "It finds the scrap's edge. If the light was bad, I can drag these handles to fix the edge myself" - drag one handle visibly, watch the cm2 number change.

**2:00-2:40 - Fit**
- Tap "Looks right - fit pieces".
- Result screen: pieces laid out inside the outline, "X of Y pieces fit", percentage used.
- "The fitting engine tries rotations and thousands of positions and never overlaps pieces. This scrap takes the pouch's outer panels but not the lining - so it tells me honestly instead of pretending."

**2:40-3:10 - Export + scrap box**
- Tap "Export real-size cutting layout". Show the print layout with the 10 cm check box.
- "This prints at true size - the grey bar must measure exactly 10 cm, then I cut along the dashed lines."
- Back, save to scrap box, open the scrap box tab.
- "Every scrap I measure is saved, so next time I can ask: what fits anything I own?"

**3:10-3:45 - What fits + close**
- Quick run: Fit tab -> "Not sure - show me what fits" -> sample or a saved scrap -> suggestion list (hexagon patchwork, patch pockets, coasters with % used).
- "It works offline, nothing leaves the phone. PatchSpan turns 'probably useless' into a plan - every scrap, fitted."

## Retake rules
- If a tap lands wrong, redo the shot - keep clips short, one per section, then join in any free editor (the phone's built-in one is fine).
- Keep the final file under the Devpost limit, upload to YouTube as unlisted OR attach directly if Devpost allows.
- Filming checklist: DND on, battery >50%, plain table, daylight, card and fabric only in frame.
